## What changed from the inference-only tutorial

The previous notebook (`TASK-INFERENCE`) segmented six phrases over the drawn scene and reported `mask_iou` / `miou` against the drawn masks as `sample-sanity`. This notebook keeps that scene and its inference contract as Section 5 and adds the adaptation contract for labelled (image, phrase, mask) records around it: a real labelled mask set fetched with per-row-group pins, a corpus-level protocol in the carried `metrics.py` (mean and micro IoU, Dice, pixel precision and recall at one threshold) with two non-adapted baselines, a bounded fine-tuning of the decoder with the upstream objective on cached CLIP activations, mask panels, the drawn scene re-segmented after the shared decoder was tuned, and an artifact that reloads. The per-image `evaluation_report` helper is unchanged; `pipe.evaluate` is the corpus-level stage (`measured` / `measured-small-sample`). The package now batches (image, phrase) pairs through the towers (`segment_batch`, `EVAL_BATCH_SIZE` pairs per forward) and accepts zip/directory BYOD with a `masks.csv`.

## Conformance notes

- **Standalone carrier (ST1–ST8):** the primary path performs no clone, repository install, or repository import. Section 2 embeds the repository's three modules verbatim in topological dependency order (each tagged `metadata.dimer.embedded_module`; the only rewrites make `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS_DIR` working-directory-relative and remove internal relative package imports), Section 3 carries the 8-file snapshot manifest inline and asserts it against the module's constants before fetching, and `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE` records the repository, revision and module digests in the exports.
- **Parity (PAR1–PAR4):** `tests/test_notebook_parity.py` and `tools/validate_release_assets.py` fail when the carried modules, the inline manifest, or the inline pins differ from the repository, or when the notebook is not byte-identical to the generator's output.
- **Model acquisition (MOD1–MOD9, MOD12):** `stage_missing_files(WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` fetches only the manifest entries a fresh runtime lacks, at the pinned revision (never `main`); `verify_snapshot` re-hashes every entry and asserts the pinned `model.safetensors` digest and byte count before the processor or the model is constructed; `ClipSegSegmentationPipeline.from_pretrained(weights_dir=WEIGHTS_DIR)` loads the verified files through `CLIPSegForImageSegmentation` / `CLIPSegProcessor` (`local_files_only=True`, `trust_remote_code=False`) in float32 and picks CUDA when it is visible. No cell outside the carried modules calls `huggingface_hub`, `transformers`, `safetensors`, `pyarrow`, `urllib`, `torch.optim` or the model's processor directly.
- **Data (DAT1–DAT9, ST6):** the default sample is FoodSeg103 (`EduardoPacheco/FoodSeg103` at the immutable parquet-conversion revision `176acc3edd2432ee126bda6fb01469eadeb018df`, Apache-2.0): the first eight row groups of `default/validation/0000.parquet` read with HTTPS range requests in the carried `samples.py` (the shard's declared size and row count checked first, each row group refused unless its SHA-256 and byte total match `ROW_GROUP_PINS`; about 43 MB), and `read_corpus` makes each image a record with its largest ingredient class as the phrase (`largest_class`, background and *other ingredients* excluded) and that class's pixels as the mask; `build_sample_dataset(seed=SPLIT_SEED)` draws 600 / 60 / 140 and `check_split_disjoint` asserts no image (by decoded-pixel digest) is shared. The template adds a second *External access* bullet naming the host, the revision, the byte count and the licence.
- **Stages (§4.1):** `fetch_corpus` + `read_corpus` + `build_sample_dataset` / `load_byod_dataset` + `split_dataset` (sample acquisition) → `validate_dataset` per split + `check_split_disjoint` + refusal probes (validation, with three digests) → `validate_inputs` with the duplicate-phrase refusal probe + `segment` on the drawn scene with structural checks + `evaluation_report` against the drawn masks (inference contract) → `empty_baseline` + `full_baseline` + `pipe.evaluate` on the frozen model with four records' scores printed (baselines) → `pipe.adapt` (bounded fine-tuning) → `pipe.evaluate` on validation and test with the comparison (evaluation) → the mask panels + the drawn scene again (inference) → `pipe.save_artifact` → `ClipSegSegmentationPipeline.from_artifact` with the parity assertion (export and reload).
- **Score semantics (UNC1–UNC4):** the per-pixel probability is an uncalibrated sigmoid and the threshold is the caller's, and the notebook says so before the first mask; the corpus rates are IoU, Dice, precision and recall at one stated threshold over one reference mask per record, with the predicted area beside them; no acceptance threshold ships. The drawn scene re-segmented after adaptation carries a `sample-sanity` verdict against masks drawn in code, and the notebook names the missing dispersion.
- **Baselines and evidence (EVAL6/EVAL10/EVAL11):** the empty-mask baseline, the full-mask baseline and the frozen model are scored on the same 140 records as the adapted model, on the same rates at the same threshold; every metric is labelled as one seeded split of one sample with no dispersion estimate, and the notebook says explicitly where the frozen model sits (build record: the frozen decoder already finds most dishes — 63 of the 140 held-out records score above 0.8 IoU — but misses 24 almost entirely (IoU under 0.2), typically ingredient names it does not ground at all (`pie`, `lamb`, `garlic`, `shellfish` at 0.0), with precision 0.835 well above recall 0.736: it under-segments) and that the gain is measured on one food dataset's largest-ingredient masks only.
- **Split integrity (SPL1–SPL8):** the split is seeded and image-disjoint; validation (60 records) selects the epoch on mean IoU and the test split is used for nothing but the final evaluation; the notebook states that images from one source, session or scene must be split together.
- **Ceilings (VAL6):** `MIN_IMAGE_SIDE` (16), `MAX_IMAGE_SIDE` (4096), `LOGIT_SIZE` (352), `MAX_PROMPTS` (16), `MAX_PROMPT_CHARS` (64), `MAX_TEXT_TOKENS` (77), `MASK_THRESHOLD` (0.5), `EXTRACT_LAYERS` (3, 6, 9), the record bounds (8..5,000), `EVAL_BATCH_SIZE` (8) and the id pattern are stated in the Prerequisites and printed before the model runs; four dataset refusals and one duplicate-phrase refusal are demonstrated.
- **BYOD (DAT10–DAT19):** `USE_BYOD` defaults to `False` so the sample path never opens an upload dialog; when enabled, one zip is read member by member (names flattened, no `extractall`) or a directory walked, each row of `masks.csv` becomes a record (the mask image's non-zero pixels are the mask), and the records flow through the same validation, image-disjoint split, baselines, fine-tuning, evaluation, export and reload cells. The expected layout and the privacy guidance are stated in the Prerequisites and in Section 4, and the training split's summary is written to `outputs/clipseg_segmentation_train.csv`.
- Licence: the weights carry the Hub's Apache-2.0 tag (the recorded discrepancy with the original code repository's statement is in `../docs/WEIGHTS.md`) and the notebook, README, card and `docs/WEIGHTS.md` say so; FoodSeg103 is Apache-2.0 (LARC-CMU-SMU; Wu et al. 2021) and is not redistributed — the export records the corpus name, repository, revision, file, licence and row groups under `corpus`.
- `tools/validate_release_assets.py` performs source validation only. It does not satisfy the clean-runtime execution requirement; a release review must confirm that a recorded clean run in `../docs/release-verification.md` matches the notebook revision under review before the status is promoted to `Release-grade`.

## Outputs

The default notebook path writes (under `outputs/`):

- `clipseg_segmentation_train.csv` — the training split's summary table (id, file names, phrase, image size, mask area fraction, provenance)
- `clipseg_segmentation_example_record.png` — the first training record's photograph with its mask overlaid

- `clipseg_segmentation_scene_frozen.json` / `.png`, `clipseg_segmentation_scene_adapted.json` / `.png` — the drawn scene's per-phrase statistics, `evaluation_report` and overlay before and after adaptation
- `clipseg_segmentation_evaluation_report.json` — baselines, frozen and adapted metrics on validation and test, the per-record rows with both IoUs, the comparison, the adaptation history
- `clipseg_segmentation_examples/` — six held-out records as panels (the photograph with the reference mask and with the adapted mask, the phrase and both IoUs)
- `clipseg_segmentation_adapter/` — `adapter.safetensors` + `manifest.json`
- `clipseg_segmentation_result.json` — `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE`, model identity, snapshot, corpus, the scene before and after adaptation with its reports, comparison, artifact digest, reload parity, runtime
